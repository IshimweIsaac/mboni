Questions

What is the difference between renting and buying?

A rental gives you 48 hours to watch from the moment you pay. Buying keeps the film in your Library forever and lets you download it for offline viewing.

How do I pay with mobile money?

Choose MTN MoMo or Airtel Money at checkout and enter your number. You'll get a prompt on your phone; approve it with your PIN and the film unlocks instantly.

How much data does a film use?

About 180 MB per hour in Data saver (240p) and 450 MB per hour in Standard (480p). Each film page shows the estimate for its runtime.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, any time from Settings. You keep access until the end of the 30 days you paid for. No hidden fees.

Is there anything for children?

Yes. Create a Kids profile and it will only show family and animated films like Anansi and the Sky Box and Imigongo.
