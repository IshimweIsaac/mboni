Name & logo options

Four directions for the platform. Each has a name with a real meaning in Kinyarwanda or Swahili, a symbol that also works as an app icon and a 32px favicon, and a tagline. None of the names is used by an existing streaming service.

Mboni

Swahili for the pupil of the eye, and used to mean “the apple of my eye”, something precious. The logo is an eye whose pupil is a play button: watching, and loving what you watch.
